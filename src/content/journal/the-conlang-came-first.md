Tonesu was the first project.

At first glance, a constructed language and a software architecture seem to ask different questions. In practice, both force decisions about categories, composition, ownership, scope, and what can safely remain implicit.

Working on Tonesu did not produce a finished software methodology all at once. The change happened gradually. Some principles were recognized only later, after the same pressure appeared in Tokimu, Tosumu, Weaver, or Anvil.

## The argument this article will make

The language work changed project design in at least four ways:

1. Names began to carry explicit semantic responsibility.
2. Composition became preferable to casual primitive expansion.
3. Corpora became evidence capable of rejecting a clean theory.
4. Sources of truth and derived representations became distinct architectural roles.

This draft will be expanded after the repository history is reviewed so that remembered influence can be separated from dated evidence.
