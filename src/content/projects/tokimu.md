## What it is

Tokimu is an experimental software system spanning rendering, resources, execution, diagnostics, and multiple frontend concerns.

## Why it matters here

Tokimu contains the clearest written form of the broader methodology: the workspace reflects semantic ownership; useful concepts are not automatically irreducible primitives; capabilities own meaning while delegating implementation; and architecture is allowed to learn from evidence.

## Questions it explores

- Who owns a meaning that crosses multiple implementations?
- What belongs in a minimal trusted core?
- When has a concept earned promotion into a stable primitive?
- How can capability discovery precede backend selection?
- How should architectural reviews, plans, notes, and ADRs relate?
