## Working definition

Primitives are expensive because everything above them inherits their worldview. Composition keeps the system able to learn.

Tonesu applies this pressure to vocabulary and semantic domains. Tokimu records it as primitive admission. Tosumu applies a related rule to public traits: stabilize only after independent callers reveal the contract.

## The test

Before adding a primitive, ask:

1. Is the meaning genuinely irreducible?
2. Can existing concepts compose without distortion?
3. What independent evidence requires permanence?
4. Which future designs would inherit this decision?
